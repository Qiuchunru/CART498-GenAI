# CART 498 – Assignment 1: Generative AI Art

## Overview

This assignment explores the use of **Generative AI for creative image generation**. The goal is to experiment with text-to-image generation and explore how prompt design can influence the visual result.

For this assignment, I created a series of three surrealist images using **Stable Diffusion** and the **Diffusers** library in Google Colab.

## Concept

The visual concept follows an elegant woman walking along a quiet European street while an enormous tree gradually transforms into a surreal house.

The three images represent a visual progression:

1. **Daytime** – A mysterious wooden door appears inside the trunk of a large tree.
2. **Dusk** – Windows, balconies, staircases, and fragments of rooms begin growing from the tree branches.
3. **Night** – The tree has transformed into a gigantic surreal house, with rooms and structures extending into the night sky as gravity begins to disappear.

The concept is inspired by **surrealism**, particularly the combination of ordinary environments with impossible objects and situations.

## Generative AI Reflection

This experiment helped me understand that generative AI is not simply an automatic image-making tool. Human decisions, especially prompt design and image selection, play an important role in the creative process. Small changes to a prompt can produce significantly different visual results.

Through this assignment, I became interested in generative AI as a creative medium that can help artists and designers explore ideas, experiment with styles, and create visual possibilities that may not have been imagined initially.
